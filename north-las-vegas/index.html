What Usually Goes Wrong in North Las Vegas Homes

Some water problems need a phone call this minute, and others can safely wait until morning. The five below are ones we see constantly in North Las Vegas, listed from most pressing to least.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Cold weather, depending on the season, is one trigger, with the leak appearing as the frozen section thaws. Shut the house supply, run a tap to drain the lines and phone us immediately, whatever the hour. Ceilings that bulge with trapped water should be left alone and the room below kept empty.

Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. This water has crossed lawns, driveways and streets, which makes it unsanitary. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. For North Las Vegas kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. When we look underneath, we tend to find water lying on the liner, insulation that is soaked or sweating ducts. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. We extract the water, dry the wood to a safe reading and tell you what we think the source is.

Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon. Since growth can start 24 to 48 hours after materials get wet, an old leak that was never fully dried is a likely culprit. Avoid wiping or bleaching a big area on your own, as that sends spores into other rooms. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across North Las Vegas.

What to Do Before Our North Las Vegas Crew Arrives

What you do before help reaches your North Las Vegas address has a real effect on how the job goes. Safety comes before saving anything. Keep out of water that touches sockets, appliances or wiring, and only cut the power if the breaker box is in a dry place you can get to safely. If the water is from a sewer or toilet backup, do not touch it at all.

Then shut the water off. Close the valve at the fixture if there is one, or the main shutoff for the house. Then move what you can carry: rugs, electronics, papers, and small furniture go to a dry room. Slide plastic or aluminum foil beneath the feet of anything too heavy to move, which stops stains and swelling.

Take photos and video of every affected room before you clean up, because your insurer will want to see the damage as it was. Mop or towel up small amounts of clean water if it is safe, and leave the rest to the crew heading to North Las Vegas.

How North Las Vegas Homeowners Can Avoid Water Damage

A large share of the jobs we attend in North Las Vegas began as a minor fault that could have been caught. Looking over a short list twice a year is enough to find most of those faults.

Start with supply lines. The rubber hoses on washing machines, dishwashers, toilets and ice makers wear out, and braided stainless replacements are inexpensive. Look under every sink for stains, swollen cabinet floors or a musty smell. At the water heater, watch for corrosion or moisture around the bottom, and flush some sediment out annually. Outside, keep gutters clear and make sure downspouts send water away from the foundation.

Know where your main shutoff valve is and make sure everyone in the house can turn it. Leak sensors placed by the water heater, washer and under sinks cost little and will alert you early. If you find something in your North Las Vegas home that worries you, such as a stain that keeps growing, have it looked at before it becomes a cleanup.
