Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house

Early Action Saves Money

Many water damage problems start small but can escalate quickly into costly emergencies. If you notice any of these signs, contact Sparks Restoration at (551) 365-8601 for a prompt evaluation.

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
