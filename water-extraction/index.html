Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Sparks Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Sparks using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

When to Call for Water Extraction in Sparks

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Program (551) 365-8601 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
